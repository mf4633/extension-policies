Board Game Turn Timer & Score Pad: privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

What the extension collects

Nothing. Everything runs on your computer. Your inputs, the date your free trial started and your Pro status are saved in your browser's extension storage so the extension remembers them. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Network use

The only network request is to ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) to check whether you have purchased Pro. It identifies the extension install, not anything you enter.

Payments

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They receive your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. The developer never sees your card number.

Selling or sharing data

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, credit or lending decisions.
