EdgeKit: privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026.

What the extension collects

Nothing. EdgeKit has no accounts, analytics, ads or servers, and it makes no network requests.

What is stored in your browser

The bets you log and your settings (currency, unit size and limits) are saved in your browser's local extension storage so the extension remembers them. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling EdgeKit deletes them, so back up first if you want to keep them.

Websites you visit

EdgeKit cannot read the websites you visit. It runs only in its own side panel.

Exports and backups

CSV exports and JSON backups are saved as files on your computer, only when you click those buttons.

Selling or sharing data

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, credit or lending decisions.
